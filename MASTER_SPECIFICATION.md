## Master Specification

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Draft

**Project Name:**
National AI Platform for Financial Fraud Response Readiness

---

# Purpose

This document is the master specification for the NAFIE AI platform.

It defines the architecture, governance, knowledge management, artificial intelligence components, deployment strategy, and implementation roadmap.

All technical documentation, source code, APIs, services, knowledge repositories, and AI agents shall conform to this specification.

---

# Vision

Build a secure, trusted, explainable, knowledge-centric AI platform that supports financial fraud response readiness in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

---

# Mission

Provide an enterprise-grade AI platform that combines:

- Knowledge Repository
- Trusted RAG
- Knowledge Graph
- Multi-Agent AI
- Explainable AI
- AI Governance
- Data Governance
- Enterprise Security
- Decision Support

---

# Guiding Principles

1. Human-Centered AI

2. Security by Design

3. Knowledge First

4. Explainability by Default

5. AI Assists, Humans Decide

6. Governance Everywhere

7. Trusted Knowledge

8. Continuous Improvement
