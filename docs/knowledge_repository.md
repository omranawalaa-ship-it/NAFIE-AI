# NAFIE AI Knowledge Repository

## 1. Purpose

The NAFIE AI Knowledge Repository is the trusted knowledge layer that supports the platform's financial fraud response readiness functions.

It provides structured, traceable, and retrievable knowledge to the AI decision-support components through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

The repository is designed to support the Saudi Arabian regulatory and operational context while allowing controlled integration of relevant international standards and frameworks.

---

## 2. Repository Objectives

The Knowledge Repository aims to:

- Provide authoritative knowledge for AI-assisted fraud response.
- Retrieve relevant regulatory and operational information for each reported case.
- Reduce unsupported AI-generated responses.
- Improve traceability and explainability.
- Preserve the source and version of retrieved information.
- Support human specialists in reviewing AI-generated recommendations.
- Enable controlled and auditable knowledge updates.

---

## 3. Knowledge Domains

The repository is organized into the following knowledge domains:

### 3.1 Laws and Regulations

Includes applicable laws, regulations, rules, and regulatory requirements relevant to financial fraud response.

### 3.2 Policies

Includes official policies, frameworks, and strategic documents relevant to financial fraud, cybersecurity, data, AI, and digital trust.

### 3.3 Procedures

Includes operational procedures, reporting requirements, response workflows, and institutional guidance.

### 3.4 Fraud Patterns

Includes structured descriptions of known financial fraud patterns, indicators, techniques, and risk signals.

### 3.5 Banking and Financial Services

Includes relevant knowledge concerning banking operations, payment services, financial transactions, and fraud-related controls.

### 3.6 Cybersecurity

Includes cybersecurity requirements, controls, incident response guidance, and relevant security frameworks.

### 3.7 AI Governance

Includes requirements and guidance related to responsible AI, governance, human oversight, transparency, and accountability.

### 3.8 International Standards

Includes selected international standards and frameworks that can provide supporting context for the platform.

---

## 4. Source Authority

Knowledge should be prioritized according to source authority.

### Priority 1 — Official Saudi Sources

Primary sources include official publications issued by:

- Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)
- National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)
- Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA)
- Official Saudi government entities
- Official Saudi regulatory authorities

### Priority 2 — Official International Sources

Where relevant, the repository may include official publications from:

- International Telecommunication Union (ITU)
- Financial Action Task Force (FATF)
- International Organization for Standardization (ISO)
- National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- Other recognized international organizations

### Priority 3 — Supporting Sources

Academic research, technical reports, and other supporting material may be included when appropriate.

Supporting sources must not override applicable official regulatory sources.
